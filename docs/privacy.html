ZH Play — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · App: ZH Play (package com.tamil.castplay) for Android phones and Android TV

In short: ZH Play has no ads, no analytics trackers, and never sells your data. It uses your Google account (name and e-mail) to let you in, and keeps a small amount of information so the app's owner can manage users, send announcements and let you control your TV. Your files, playlists and media stay on your devices.

1. Who we are

ZH Play is developed by Tamilarasan ("we", "the developer"). Contact: tamilarasan5333@gmail.com.

3. Permissions and what they are used for

  • Storage / all files access — to play and manage your videos, music and files, including SD cards and USB drives. Files are read on your device only and are never uploaded by ZH Play. When you choose to cast or share a file to your own TV, it is sent directly over your local Wi-Fi.
  • Local network — to find your TV on the same Wi-Fi and control it.
  • Microphone — only when you tap a voice button. Speech is turned into text by your device's speech service (for example Google); ZH Play does not record or store audio.
  • Notifications — for announcements, live broadcasts and media controls.
  • Accessibility service (TV, optional) — when you enable it on your TV, ZH Play uses it to press keys and type text that you send from your phone remote. It is not used to collect personal information.
  • VPN (optional) — when you turn on the built-in WireGuard VPN, your traffic goes through the VPN server you choose. ZH Play does not log or inspect your traffic.
  • Camera — not used by ZH Play for sign-in; to sign in a TV you scan its QR code with your phone's own camera app.

4. Services from other companies

  • Google — Sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Cloud Messaging. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub — the app downloads its public settings and app updates from GitHub. GitHub may log normal technical information such as your IP address. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • AI assistant (optional) — if you add your own API key, your questions are sent to the AI service you chose (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Groq or OpenRouter) under that service's privacy policy. Your key is stored encrypted on your device only.
  • Seedr (optional) — if you sign in to your own Seedr account, ZH Play talks to Seedr on your behalf. Your Seedr sign-in is stored on your device only. See Seedr's privacy policy.
  • Websites and streams you open — the built-in browser and player connect to the sites and links you choose, under their own policies.

5. How long we keep information

Your user record stays while you use ZH Play and is removed when you ask us to delete it (section 7). Cloud remote commands are deleted right after use. Broadcast viewing records are kept for the owner's statistics and are removed on request. Information on your device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

6. Security

Data in our Firebase database is protected by Google sign-in and access rules: each person can only read and write their own private data, and only the app owner can read the user list. Connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Delete your data / your account

You can delete your data at any time:

  • On your device: in ZH Play open the Account tile → Sign out. Uninstalling the app removes everything ZH Play stored on the device.
  • From our database: e-mail tamilarasan5333@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject "ZH Play – delete my data". We delete your user record, permissions, TV information and broadcast viewing records within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.
  • You can also remove ZH Play's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Children

ZH Play is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Contact us at the e-mail above and we will help.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes may also be announced in the app.
